Regional Pricing Policy v2.3
Effective: January 1, 2024

Standard Pricing:
Standard pricing applies uniformly across all regions in India.
All prices are exclusive of GST (18% applicable on hardware, 18% on software).

Discount Authority:
Regional discounts may be approved by the Regional VP for strategic situations:
- Orders above INR 10L: up to 5% discount (Regional Manager approval)
- Strategic accounts: up to 10% discount (Regional VP approval)
- Competitive situations: up to 15% discount (Country Head approval)
- Government/Education: up to 20% discount (Country Head + CFO approval)

No discount exceeding 15% may be offered without written CFO approval.

Competitive Response Policy:
When a competitor offers pricing more than 10% below our list price,
sales representatives should escalate to their Regional VP within 48 hours.
A competitive pricing response may be authorized for up to 3 months
while the competitive threat is assessed.

Payment Terms:
Standard payment terms are Net-30 from invoice date.
For new customers, advance payment of 30% is required before shipment.
Enterprise accounts with 12+ months history may request Net-45.

Price Protection:
Customers who have placed a purchase order at a quoted price are
protected from price increases for 60 days from the order date.
