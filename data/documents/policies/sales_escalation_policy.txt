SALES ESCALATION AND DEAL REVIEW POLICY
CortexFlow Enterprise — Sales Operations Division
Version 3.0 | Effective: March 2024
==========================================

PURPOSE
-------
This policy governs when and how sales opportunities, at-risk accounts, and revenue performance issues must be escalated within the organization. It ensures timely executive visibility and intervention when needed.

SCOPE
-----
Applies to all Account Managers, Regional Sales Managers, CSMs, and Sales Leadership across all South India territories including Kerala, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh.

ESCALATION THRESHOLDS
---------------------

1. OPPORTUNITY ESCALATION (upward deal support)
   Escalate to Regional Sales Manager when:
   - Single deal value exceeds INR 5,00,000
   - Customer is requesting multi-year pricing not in the standard rate card
   - Competitive situation where a competitor has submitted a formal proposal
   - Customer's procurement committee includes C-level decision-makers

   Escalate to VP of Sales when:
   - Single deal value exceeds INR 25,00,000
   - Strategic partnership or OEM/reseller discussions begin
   - Government or PSU procurement (different compliance requirements)

2. REVENUE DECLINE ESCALATION (territory under-performance)
   Escalate to Regional Sales Manager when:
   - Territory revenue declines more than 10% quarter-over-quarter
   - A single account's revenue drops more than 25% in a quarter
   - Two or more accounts in the same territory show simultaneous decline

   CURRENTLY TRIGGERED: Kerala territory — Q3 2024 decline of 16.8% YoY requires
   immediate Regional Sales Manager review and documented action plan.

   Escalate to VP of Sales when:
   - Territory revenue declines more than 20% quarter-over-quarter
   - Decline persists for two or more consecutive quarters

   CURRENTLY TRIGGERED: Kerala Electronics decline trend (Q1→Q2→Q3 2024) requires
   VP of Sales review and strategic intervention plan.

3. CUSTOMER HEALTH ESCALATION (churn risk)
   See Customer Churn Prevention Policy v2.1 for full escalation matrix.
   Quick reference:
   - MEDIUM risk (CHS 50-74): RSM notified within 5 days
   - HIGH risk (CHS 0-49): VP of Sales notified within 24 hours

DEAL REVIEW CADENCE
-------------------
Weekly:
  - Deals above INR 2,00,000 reviewed in Regional Pipeline Review call
  - HIGH churn risk accounts reviewed — mandatory attendance by RSM

Monthly:
  - Full territory business review (TBR) with RSM + all AMs
  - Customer health dashboard reviewed for all accounts
  - Competitive win/loss analysis presented

Quarterly:
  - Executive Business Review (EBR) with VP of Sales
  - QBRs offered to all LOW risk customers with >INR 10,00,000 annual revenue
  - Territory performance vs. plan — corrective action required if >15% below target

DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------
All escalations must be logged in the CRM within 24 hours with:
1. Account/opportunity name
2. Escalation reason (specific policy trigger)
3. Current status and risk assessment
4. Proposed action plan with owners and timelines
5. Expected resolution date

APPROVAL AUTHORITY TABLE
------------------------
Discount up to 5%:          Account Manager (self-approval)
Discount 6-10%:             Regional Sales Manager
Discount 11-15%:            VP of Sales
Discount above 15%:         CEO approval required
Extended payment >Net-60:   CFO sign-off required
Retention offers (policy):  Regional Sales Manager
