GROWTH DRIVERS
--------------
1. Bengaluru Tech Ecosystem: The city's startup and enterprise tech boom continues to drive hardware and software procurement. Karnataka has over 12,000 active tech companies — our highest density territory.

2. Government Digital India Push: Karnataka's IT/BT Policy 2020-2025 includes significant cloud migration mandates for state departments, creating new software subscription opportunities.

3. Cloud Platform Adoption: Cloud Platform subscriptions in Karnataka grew 22% in Q3 2024, driven by BLR001's expansion and three new mid-market accounts in the pipeline.

4. Proactive Account Management: Karnataka's dedicated account manager ran two in-person business reviews in Q3, directly contributing to the BLR001 expansion order.
