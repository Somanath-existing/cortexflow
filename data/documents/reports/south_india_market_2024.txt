South India Market Analysis — 2024

Market Overview:
South India represents 23% of total company revenue.
Karnataka is performing well with 5.8% Electronics growth and 21.9% Software growth.
Kerala experienced a 16.8% overall revenue decline in Q3 2024.
Tamil Nadu remained stable with approximately 5% growth.

Revenue by State (Q3 2024, all categories combined):
- Karnataka: INR 73.3L (+5.8% YoY)
- Tamil Nadu: INR 29.5L (+5.4% YoY)
- Kerala: INR 49.5L (-16.8% YoY)
- Telangana: INR 38.2L (+3.1% YoY)

Competitive Landscape in Kerala:
LocalTech Solutions entered Kerala in Q2 2024. They have focused on commodity
hardware and are building relationships with mid-market customers. Their pricing
strategy of 15-20% below market is likely unsustainable long-term, but they
are gaining short-term traction. We have not seen them target software or SaaS.

IT Sector Slowdown Impact:
The broader IT sector slowdown in India during Q2-Q3 2024 affected enterprise
spending across verticals. Kerala's IT sector, concentrated in Kochi and
Trivandrum, was particularly affected due to its exposure to global delivery
projects that saw budget cuts from US and European clients.

Customer Segmentation:
Enterprise (>INR 5L annual revenue): 3 accounts in Kerala — all showing decline.
Mid-market (INR 1-5L annual revenue): 5 accounts — mixed performance.
SMB (<INR 1L annual revenue): 12 accounts — relatively stable.

Growth Opportunities:
1. SaaS transition: Kerala's IT workforce is highly skilled and cloud-ready.
2. Government projects: Kerala state IT mission has budget for digital infrastructure.
3. Education sector: Several engineering colleges are expanding IT infrastructure.
