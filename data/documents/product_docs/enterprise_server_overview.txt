PRODUCT SUMMARY
---------------
The Enterprise Server Pro is our flagship on-premise compute solution designed for mid-to-large enterprise customers with demanding workloads. It is the top-selling hardware product in our South India portfolio.

List Price: INR 2,50,000 per unit (ex-GST)
Category: Electronics / Enterprise Hardware
Product Code: ESP-4U-2024

TARGET CUSTOMERS
----------------
- Enterprise accounts (>500 employees) requiring on-premise compute
- Government and PSU organizations with data residency requirements
- Financial institutions, healthcare providers, and manufacturing companies
- IT/ITES companies running high-performance workloads

KEY SPECIFICATIONS
------------------
Processor:      Dual Intel Xeon Gold 6348 (28 cores each, 56 cores total)
Memory:         512GB DDR4 ECC RAM (expandable to 2TB)
Storage:        4x 3.84TB NVMe SSD (15.36TB total, RAID 10)
Network:        4x 25GbE + 2x 10GbE management
Power:          Dual redundant 1600W PSU (80 Plus Titanium)
Form Factor:    4U Rack Mount
Warranty:       3-year on-site next business day
Operating Temp: 10°C to 35°C

USE CASES
---------
1. Virtualization Host: Supports up to 200 VMs with VMware ESXi or Hyper-V
2. Database Server: Optimized for Oracle, SQL Server, and PostgreSQL high-availability clusters
3. AI/ML Inference: GPU expansion slots support NVIDIA A100/H100 (sold separately)
4. ERP Hosting: Certified for SAP HANA, Oracle ERP Cloud on-premise deployments
